# 🚢 AI-Driven Freight Forecasting & Vessel Procurement Platform

An end-to-end intelligent decision-support platform built for the **Ministry of Steel (Government of India)** to optimize bulk raw material imports, vessel chartering, and maritime logistics along India's Eastern Seaboard (Problem Statement: **SIH26006**).

---

## 📌 Overview

Importing bulk cargo (such as coking coal and iron ore) from overseas suppliers involves high market volatility, unpredictable ocean freight rates, and costly port demurrage delays. 

This platform leverages predictive machine learning models and Google AI Studio to forecast spot rate trends, estimate inflation impact, integrate voyage weather conditions, and recommend optimal vessel booking windows—enabling cost-efficient procurement and vessel chartering.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **📉 Freight Rate & Spot Rate Inflation Forecasting:** Predicts ocean bulk shipping rates and spot rate inflation trends across global routes using historical Baltic Dry Index (BDI) patterns.
- **🧠 Smart Chartering & Booking Recommender:** Suggests the most cost-effective vessel booking windows to minimize charter costs and prevent port demurrage fees.
- **🌤️ Maritime Weather & Route Intelligence:** Integrates real-time ocean weather patterns to assess voyage risks, fuel consumption impact, and realistic transit time estimates.
- **⚓ Vendor & Chartering Directory:** Matches procurement requests with top-tier global vessel charterers (Capesize/Panamax) and overseas raw material suppliers with live reliability and transit scoring.
- **📊 Executive Dashboard:** Interactive visual analytics displaying route cost comparisons, market trends, and automated buying recommendations.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & AI Architecture

- **Core AI Engine:** Google AI Studio (Gemini)
- **Prompt Engineering:** System Instructions with Strict JSON Schema Grounding (Zero-Hallucination Guardrails)
- **Data Integrations:** Real-time Freight & Weather Data Reasoning, Spot Rate Inflation Estimation
- **Frontend / Interface:** Built via AI Studio Web App Framework

---

## 🎯 Target Impact

- **Cost Savings:** Up to 10–15% reduction in vessel chartering expenses by hitting ideal booking windows.
- **Demurrage Elimination:** Minimizes port waiting times across East Coast Indian ports (Visakhapatnam, Paradip, Haldia, Dhamra).
- **Data-Driven Procurement:** Replaces manual guesswork with predictive machine learning insights for government and enterprise buyers.
